Featured Events

  1. Coding Challenge

    Test your programming skills in our Coding Challenge. Compete against fellow coders to solve complex problems and win exciting prizes.

    Rules

    1. Participants must be registered for TechFest 2026.
    2. Each team can have a maximum of 4 members.
    3. Problems will be released at the start of the challenge.

  2. Robotics Competitions

    Build and program robots to compete in our exciting robotics competitions. Showcase your engineering skills and creativity.

    Rules

    1. Participants must be registered for TechFest 2026.
    2. Teams can have a maximum of 5 members.
    3. Robots must adhere to the specified size and weight limits.
    4. Competitions will be held in various categories based on robot capabilities.

  3. Web Development Workshop

    Learn the latest web development techniques and tools in our hands-on workshop. Gain practical experience from industry experts.

    Rules

    1. Participants must be registered for TechFest 2026.
    2. Workshop is open to all skill levels, from beginners to advanced developers.
    3. Participants are encouraged to bring their own laptops for hands-on exercises.

  4. AI Quiz

    Test your knowledge of artificial intelligence in our interactive quiz. Challenge yourself with questions on machine learning, neural networks, and more.

    Rules

    1. Participants must be registered for TechFest 2026.
    2. Quiz will consist of multiple-choice questions covering various AI topics.
    3. Top scorers will receive exciting prizes.

  5. Hackathon

    Collaborate with fellow tech enthusiasts in our 24-hour hackathon. Create innovative solutions to real-world problems and present your ideas to a panel of judges.

    Rules

    1. Participants must be registered for TechFest 2026.
    2. Teams can have a maximum of 5 members.
    3. Projects must be developed during the hackathon period.
    4. Final presentations will be judged based on creativity, functionality, and impact.

  6. Cognitive Computing Challenge

    Explore the world of cognitive computing in this unique challenge. Develop applications that mimic human thought processes and compete for recognition.

    Rules

    1. Participants must be registered for TechFest 2026.
    2. Teams can have a maximum of 4 members.
    3. Applications must utilize cognitive computing techniques to solve a specified problem.
    4. Submissions will be evaluated based on innovation, functionality, and user experience.

  7. Cybersecurity Challenge

    Test your cybersecurity skills in this thrilling challenge. Identify vulnerabilities, defend against attacks, and secure systems to win exciting prizes.

    Rules

    1. Participants must be registered for TechFest 2026.
    2. Teams can have a maximum of 4 members.
    3. Challenges will include tasks such as penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, and incident response.
    4. Top performers will receive recognition and prizes.

  8. Data Science Competition

    Showcase your data science skills in this competitive event. Analyze complex datasets, derive insights, and present your findings to a panel of judges.

    Rules

    1. Participants must be registered for TechFest 2026.
    2. Teams can have a maximum of 4 members.
    3. Datasets will be provided at the start of the competition, and participants will have a set time to analyze and present their findings.
    4. Submissions will be evaluated based on the quality of insights, methodology, and presentation.

  9. Bug Hunt

    Put your debugging skills to the test in our Bug Hunt challenge. Identify and fix bugs in a simulated software environment to win exciting prizes.

    Rules

    1. Participants must be registered for TechFest 2026.
    2. Teams can have a maximum of 4 members.
    3. Participants will be given access to a simulated software environment with pre-injected bugs. The goal is to identify and fix as many bugs as possible within the allotted time.
    4. Submissions will be evaluated based on the number of bugs fixed, accuracy, and efficiency.
